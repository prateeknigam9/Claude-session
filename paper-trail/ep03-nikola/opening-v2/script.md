# Case 03 · new opening test (v2) — 2026-11-08

**Goal:** make the first ~65 seconds of Case 03 in a new style, then put it next to the current opening so you can judge the difference before we change the whole process.

## What changes, and why

| | Current opening (v1) | New opening (v2) |
|---|---|---|
| **Point of view** | A narrator above the story, listing facts | We sit with the people who uncovered it: a small research team, months into digging |
| **The ending** | Given away in the first minute: billionaire, guilty, 4 years, pardon | Held back. We only tease "a courtroom, a bankruptcy, and a phone call from the White House" |
| **Structure** | Fact, fact, fact, all at the same intensity | One question ("what's wrong with this road?"), a reveal, then a choice with stakes ("publish, or stay quiet?") |
| **Hedging** | "According to prosecutors…" in almost every line | One clear attribution ("according to their sources"); the legal care stays in the on-screen tags |
| **Numbers** | $30B, more than Ford, 0 trucks, billionaire, 4 years… | Two numbers only: $30 billion and $2 billion |
| **Pictures** | Cards slide in, stamps slam, labels everywhere | Staged scenes: a dark office at night, the video on a monitor, the frame tilting level to show the hill, a finger over "publish". Almost no text on screen |
| **Voice** | Kokoro, the same energy on every line | An expressive narrator who drops to a near-whisper, then pauses, then hits |
| **Sound** | A paper slam every few seconds | Mostly room tone, a ticking clock and a low drone; silence before the reveal; one hit on "It's not flat." |

## The script (about 175 words, ~65 s with the pauses)

> `[low, close to the mic]`
> September, twenty twenty. New York.
>
> A small team of researchers has been digging into one company for months.
> Old videos. Internal messages. Former employees, finally talking.
>
> And one video they keep coming back to.
>
> A huge white truck, gliding down a desert road. Silent. Smooth. The future.
>
> The company behind it is worth about thirty billion dollars. More than Ford.
> And General Motors has just agreed to buy a two-billion-dollar piece of it.
>
> But the researchers have noticed something about that road.
>
> `[pause]`
>
> It's not flat.
>
> `[pause]`
>
> According to their sources, the truck wasn't driving at all.
> Someone had towed it to the top of a hill… and let it roll.
>
> So now they have a choice.
> Publish… and take on one of the hottest companies in America, and the biggest carmaker in the country.
> Or stay quiet.
>
> `[beat]`
>
> On September tenth, they hit publish.
>
> What happened next would end in a courtroom… a bankruptcy… and a phone call from the White House.
>
> This is the paper trail. Case three. The Hill.

## Pictures, shot by shot (staged, not slideshow)

1. **Night office.** A dark paper office; one desk lamp, a wall clock ticking, rain on the window. Two generic researcher puppets, seen from behind, at a cluttered desk. The camera drifts in slowly.
2. **The pile.** Their hands spread out evidence: a stack of printouts, a phone with message bubbles, a polaroid with its face hidden ("former employee"). No labels, just objects.
3. **The monitor.** The camera pushes into the monitor until the truck video fills the frame. It plays: smooth, silent, golden light.
4. **The stakes.** Back in the office, a newspaper lands on the desk: a $30B headline and the GM deal. The researchers look at each other.
5. **The reveal.** Back inside the video, the frame slowly **rotates until the horizon is level**, and the "flat" road turns out to be a steep slope. Silence, then one low hit on "It's not flat."
6. **How.** A quick paper reconstruction: a tow rope, the truck at the top, a hand lets go, and it rolls. One small tag: SOURCES · HINDENBURG REPORT.
7. **The choice.** A close-up of a finger hovering over a key marked PUBLISH. The clock ticks louder. Two big shadow shapes loom over the desk: a carmaker's tower and a stock chart.
8. **Publish.** The key goes down; a printer roars; pages fly across the screen and become the case board.
9. **The tease.** Three quick silhouettes on the board: a gavel, a door stamped BANKRUPT, a phone ringing in the dark. Hard cut to the title card: THE PAPER TRAIL · CASE 03 · THE HILL.

## Legal and accuracy notes

- **Office scene:** the office night scene is a reconstruction. Hindenburg's report did cite former employees and internal messages, but we don't know what their office looked like. A small "dramatization" tag sits in the corner of shots 1–4.
- **Attribution:** the claim about the truck is attributed once, to "their sources", plus the on-screen tag. Nikola itself later said it "never stated" the truck was driving under its own power.
- **Not on screen:** no names or faces of the researchers. No real logos: GM, Ford and Nikola appear as plain type.
- **Two facts to confirm:** that the report was published on Sept 10 2020, and that the GM deal was announced on Sept 8 2020, two days earlier. Both are already confirmed in the Case 03 fact table.
